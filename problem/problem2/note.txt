ner is not 100% accurate.
POS tells us:"this is an adjective"
Dependency tells us:"this adjective is related to this word"
The key point is:amod is not the only way an adjective can be related to a noun.
amod — adjective directly modifies a noun (terrible service)
acomp — adjective is a verb's complement via a copula ("was amazing")
attr — adjective/noun as attribute in a copular sentence
nsubj / nsubjpass — subject / passive subject of a verb
appos — an appositive (a renaming phrase: "the waiter, Mike,")
dobj, pobj — direct object, object of a preposition.
